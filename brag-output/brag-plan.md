# Brag Plan: SparkStack (design 0018)

## Step 1 rubric

1. **What is it?** SparkStack's layered wall art: 154 laser-cut acrylic pieces stacked in 10 layers on standoffs, forming a 3D Spider-Man relief that hangs on your wall.
2. **Most impressive claim:** the construction itself, with every number read from the model file: 154 laser-cut pieces, 10 layers, 54 standoffs, and one finished image. "154 pieces, 10 layers" is the brag.
3. **Visual hook:** a cloud of loose, glossy, colored pieces that snaps into a hero, layer by layer.
4. **What to show from the real thing:** the real GLB (`spidy_small.glb`), rendered live in the composition. That covers the pieces, the layer stack, the standoffs and caps, and the real material colors. Nothing is re-drawn or approximated.
5. **Shortest satisfying video:** about 20 s. The assembly needs roughly 4 s to read, and the depth shot and the room reveal each need one hold.
6. **Tone:** preset `default`. Creative direction: *"pop-art product drop: a hero assembles itself, piece by piece, on a sunlit wall."*
7. **Audio feel:** an upbeat, clean bed (vol-9, mid-energy, about 115 BPM). Plastic chip clacks as layers seat, a warm success hit on the payoff, and restrained accents elsewhere.
8. **Share caption:** "154 laser-cut acrylic pieces. 10 layers. 1 hero, on your wall. SparkStack: art that stacks up."
9. **User flow worth showing:** a physical product has no app flow, so the equivalent is its life cycle: loose cut pieces → layers stacked on standoffs → hanging finished on a wall at home. That's the centerpiece.

## What is this?
Layered laser-cut acrylic wall art. The piece shown is a 3D Spider-Man built from 154 cut pieces in 10 layers.

## The angle
Count it up. The video literally counts the pieces as they appear (154), then the layers as they seat (10), then lands on the payoff, "1 hero." The numbers are true and specific to this piece, and the countdown-to-one structure makes the construction the joke and the brag at once. Then it proves the depth, puts it on a wall, and names the brand.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
Colored acrylic pieces pop into the air one by one in front of a warm, sunlit wall. The headline itself is the counter: **"001 pieces."** races up in step with the pops and settles on **"154 pieces."** at 1.45 s.

## Key moments
- The layers seat onto the standoffs one at a time, bottom first, with a readout ticking LAYER 01/10 … 10/10 and the headline **"10 layers."**
- The payoff: the last layer seats, the caps pop, the image resolves, and **"1 hero."** lands on the strongest beat.
- A raking side view along the edge: stepped layers, standoffs and cast shadows, with **"Real depth." "Not a print."**

## Outro / punchline
The camera pulls back to reveal the piece hanging above a console in a sunlit room: **"Made for your wall."** Then the lockup: **SparkStack**, *Art that stacks up.*, sparkstack.com.

## User flow worth showing
Loose pieces → stacked layers → hanging on a wall. There's no software UI; the flow is the product's own assembly and use, shown with the real model.

## Tone
- Preset: `default`
- Creative direction: pop-art product drop, a hero assembling itself on a sunlit wall
- Interpretation: punchy, warm, playful. Big heavy display type that slams in and then holds. Soft camera-driven transitions, with one hard cut on a strong beat into the depth shot. Five scenes with room to breathe.
